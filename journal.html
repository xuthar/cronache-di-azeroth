Travel Journal · 10 November 2026 · The Road of Ashes

Two Friends. Two Continents. One Unforgettable Road.

Xuthar Morvayne · Human Shadow Priest · Level 17 — An evening that began with a mysterious note among Westfall’s ashes became the most ambitious expedition of his young life. At the crossroads between Redridge and Duskwood, Xuthar joined Carro Steelcave. Together they followed the trail through the forest and the southern harbour, across the Great Sea to Kalimdor, through the Barrens and the heights of Stonetalon, then back to Loch Modan and the broken heights of Thoradin’s Wall.

What the first clue promised was never wealth or glory. The long chain of six quests led to the Cozy Sleeping Bag: a humble reward made extraordinary by the distance travelled to find it. Between the beginning and the end, Xuthar grew from level 15 to level 17, crossing landscapes he had never seen and learning what it meant to trust a friend in places where even a single careless step might be costly.

Carro did not merely accompany the priest; he shared the uncertain roads, mountain climbs and quiet victories. These are the moments that endure beyond any tally of experience. The completed expedition is preserved here in eighty-eight original photographs, and transformed into a continuous literary adventure in The Last Witness, Chapters LIV–LIX.

Earlier journal · 8–9 November. At Westfall, difficult quests and new allies brought Xuthar to level 15. He reunited with Carro for an ale in Goldshire and a walk through Stormwind, then rested at the Gilded Rose. On 9 November, he returned to Westfall, fished beneath the rain, completed further difficult work, and spent an exhausted evening reflecting at Sentinel Hill. Those quiet hours led to the courage of the next day.
