XUTHAR’S STORY · ORIGINAL ROLEPLAY

He could not bring an army. He could bring himself.

Yesterday’s Messenger to Darkshire chain ended with a difficult answer: Ebonlocke could not lend the Night Watch to Lakeshire. After the victory at Stonewatch, The Last Witness gives Xuthar a reason to return. The town that had seemed unable to help was itself waiting for help.

For a Shadow Priest who keeps the names of forgotten people, Duskwood offers more than another battlefield. Sirra’s search for the Valor family’s history echoes his own purpose; Stalvan’s name opens an investigation; Althea’s orders turn compassion into work. The greater mysteries remain beyond the lamps.

Eight new chapters begin with the square beneath the clock and end at the threshold of the next road. The arrival and quest states come from the game record; personal motives and dialogue are the novel’s interpretation.

THE REGION · HISTORY AND SHADOWS

Before Duskwood, there was Brightwood.

South of Elwynn, this was once a settled, fertile part of the kingdom of Stormwind. Brightwood and the town once called Grand Hamlet survive in the history behind today’s Duskwood and Darkshire. A traveler arriving now finds trees bent into dark shapes, abandoned farms and settlements that have learned to keep their lamps burning.

Warcraft’s wider history connects the land’s corruption with the fel energies released at Medivh’s death. That history is one layer of the region’s troubles. Necromancy, the Nightbane worgen and the mystery of the Scythe of Elune have their own threads; a single neat explanation would erase much of what makes this forest unsettling.

Darkshire survives under Lord Ello Ebonlocke, with Commander Althea Ebonlocke’s Night Watch guarding the roads. Stormwind has withdrawn its commissioned guards, leaving local people to defend themselves. This is why yesterday’s request for reinforcements could not be answered—and why the next defender to arrive matters.

The story here is not only about the creatures in the trees. It is also about people who remain: a commander organizing another patrol, a cook needing ingredients, a historian looking for a lost book, and families who must carry on living beside unquiet graves.

This guide follows the region and quest setting relevant to Xuthar’s Forever journey. Later expansion events are outside this chapter.

WHAT LIES IN THE WOODS

A town, two cemeteries, and roads full of stories.

Darkshire
The inhabited eastern town, with the clock tower, town hall, inn, library and Night Watch. This is Xuthar’s recorded arrival location.
Tranquil Gardens
The cemetery south of Darkshire. The first Night Watch commission sends Xuthar against its skeletal warriors and mages.
Raven Hill & Raven Hill Cemetery
The western ruins and burial grounds draw several new assignments: an investigation, a search for the Valor tome and the way toward isolated residents.
Brightwood Grove & the Rotting Orchard
Old, gentler names now stand beside the Nightbane worgen threat. Calor’s commission asks for six Shadow Weavers.
Twilight Grove
A great tree and a portal connected to the Emerald Dream occupy the heart of the region. It is part of Duskwood’s geography, not a newly recorded visit by Xuthar.
The roads beyond the border
Deadwind Pass lies eastward and Stranglethorn southward. Today’s Stalvan clue instead points back to Moonbrook in Westfall.
Sven’s camp & the hermit’s shack
Two different destinations near the western cemetery. One needs supplies; the other calls for a welfare visit. Both introductory errands are now complete; their later requests continue in the 22 November update.
Rumours of greater dangers
Morbent Fel’s necromancy and the road-haunting abomination Stitches belong to Duskwood’s larger story. They are not victories or encounters added to Xuthar’s record today.
