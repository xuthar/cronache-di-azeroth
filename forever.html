13 NOVEMBER 2026 · LEVEL 19 · THE CRAFT BETWEEN ADVENTURES

Thread, dust and patient hands

Victory beneath Westfall was one part of the journey. Away from the fighting, Xuthar has been learning how to mend, provide and prepare. All five professions now have a 150 skill cap; their current values are recorded below.

Tailoring 77/150

JOURNEYMAN

From the first linen stitches and the bags made for the road, Xuthar has passed the apprentice ceiling and reached 77.

Next steps: Aim first for 100, then 125. Save wool, compare available patterns and make useful bags or cloth gear when their recipe colour still offers progress. Reserve part of the cloth for First Aid.

Enchanting 90/150

JOURNEYMAN

After leaving Herbalism behind, Xuthar learned to recover magic from unwanted equipment. His second craft now leads at 90.

Next steps: Aim for 100, then 125. Disenchant spare eligible items after checking their value; choose useful orange or yellow enchants with affordable materials. Check rod requirements before buying reagents.

Cooking 84/150

JOURNEYMAN

Cooking has grown alongside the journey, reaching 84: the quiet craft that turns provisions into a reason to pause.

Next steps: Work toward 100 using recipes for fish and meat already in the bags. Learn suitable recipes before gathering ingredients, and keep useful food for the next expedition.

Fishing 75/150

JOURNEYMAN

The rainy waters of Westfall belong to this story as surely as its battles. Fishing stands at 75, with the next rank already unlocked.

Next steps: Continue toward 100 in manageable waters. Keep fish that match known Cooking recipes; use bait when needed and let the two skills advance together.

First Aid 73/150

JOURNEYMAN

A new practical discipline now accompanies the priest: First Aid is at 73, and its Journeyman training already leaves room beyond 75.

Next steps: Reach 75 first, then work toward 100. Check the trainer for available bandages and use recipes that still grant skill, balancing cloth supplies with Tailoring.

Beyond these first goals, work toward 125 and check each profession’s next-rank requirements before reaching 150. A higher skill cap does not mean that its recipes have already been learned. These are plans, not completed milestones.
